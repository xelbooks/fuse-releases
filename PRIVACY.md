# Fuse for Android — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 18 August 2026
**Publisher:** Xelbooks LLC d/b/a Brandenacity
**Contact:** team@brandenacity.com

## The short version

Fuse has no account, no analytics and no server of ours in the middle. We do not
collect your personal information, and we have nothing to sell.

## What Fuse stores, and where

Everything Fuse knows about you stays on your device, in the app's own storage:

- your groups and the sites in them
- your settings — theme, text size, accessibility choices, search engine
- the place you set for the forecast, and the cities in your world clock
- anything you write in the safety plan inside "A moment to breathe"
- cookies and site data for the websites you sign in to

None of it is transmitted to us. Uninstalling Fuse removes all of it.

## What leaves your device, and only if you ask

Fuse itself makes two kinds of request, and neither happens until you turn it on:

1. **Headlines** — if you pick topics in My News, Fuse asks those publishers'
   public feeds for their headlines. Nothing about you goes with the request.
2. **Weather** — if you set a place, Fuse asks Open-Meteo
   (https://open-meteo.com) for the forecast for that place. The request carries
   the coordinates of the town you chose and nothing else. No account, no key.

Everything else that leaves your device is you visiting a website. Those sites
see what any browser would show them, and their own privacy policies apply.

## Fay

Fay, the built-in guide, runs entirely on your device. Her answers are written
into the app. Nothing you ask her is sent anywhere, and nothing you ask her is
stored.

## "A moment to breathe"

The calm corner records nothing and sends nothing. A safety plan you write there
is kept on your device only, and you can clear it from the same screen.

## Children

Fuse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone.

## Permissions

Fuse asks for internet access, which a browser needs. It does not ask for your
contacts, your location (the forecast uses the town you type, not device GPS),
your camera or your microphone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
